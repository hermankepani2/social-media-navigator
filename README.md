

# Social Media Navigator

Social Media Navigator is a human-led AI assistant for planning, drafting, reviewing, and improving social media content. It helps users turn ideas into platform-appropriate posts while keeping final judgment, approval, publishing, and engagement decisions with the human user.

AI proposes. The human steersman decides.

## Project Principles

- Human-led decision making
- Human review before publishing
- Platform-appropriate content
- Responsible AI use
- Clear separation between AI assistance and human approval
